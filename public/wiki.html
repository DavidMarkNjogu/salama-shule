2. The Incumbent LMS Fallacy: Why Moodle & Canvas Fail

Traditional Learning Management Systems assume Western conditions: high-speed fiber internet and continuous connectivity. In Kenya, when cellular connections drop, traditional LMS sessions abort and student submissions are lost.

3. Zero-Trust Assessment Architecture

Why the Bank Teller Counts Cash

When a student takes a quiz offline, the browser displays a score estimate for feedback. However, cynical judges ask: "Can't an engineering student open browser DevTools and alter the score variable to 100%?"

Server-Side Re-Marking Protocol:

When the student reconnects, their device transmits raw option selections (e.g. [1, 0, 2]), not the final grade. The server independently marks every answer against the database answer key. If a student altered their local browser storage to claim 100%, the server discards the client claim, computes the authentic score, and logs the attempt into the lecturer gradebook.
