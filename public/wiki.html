Module 1: The Government Monopoly Myth (KNEC vs ChetiCheck)

A common misconception during pitch sessions is that the government already runs an official verification website with the national database. In reality, educational jurisdiction in Kenya is divided by law into two separate realms:

KNEC QMIS Portal (qmis.knec.ac.ke)
  • Jurisdiction: Primary & Secondary only (KCPE / KCSE).
  • University Mandate: Zero database records for universities.
  • Turnaround Time: 10 to 14 working days (manual search).
  • Cost to Verify: KES 2,280 - KES 2,320 per physical letter.
  • Mechanism: Scanned paper copies sent via courier.
ChetiCheck Platform (cheticheck.vercel.app)
  • Jurisdiction: Higher Education (Universities, Diplomas, TVET).
  • University Mandate: Issued directly by Registrar signing keys.
  • Turnaround Time: Sub-200 milliseconds (instant scan).
  • Cost to Verify: Free for graduates, KES 250 corporate API.
  • Mechanism: Asymmetric Ed25519 cryptographic signature.

Module 2: Centralized Database Honeypots vs Asymmetric Cryptography

Why isn't a traditional SQL database enough? If an insider is bribed or a database is breached, traditional SQL records can be altered without detection. Asymmetric cryptography creates a mathematical seal that cannot be forged.

The Centralized SQL Database Risk

In traditional university portals, trust depends 100% on the server host. If a database administrator executes UPDATE degrees SET class = 'First Class', the API will falsely report the forged degree as genuine.

The ChetiCheck Cryptographic Solution

ChetiCheck creates an Ed25519 asymmetric signature over a SHA-256 digest of the canonical record. Even if a rogue DBA alters the database table, the mathematical signature breaks immediately upon employer scan.

Module 3: The Incumbent LMS Myth (Moodle vs Salama Shule)

Moodle and Canvas have native apps, but Kenyan students frequently uninstall them. Why? Storage and bandwidth limits on entry-level Android devices.

Moodle Mobile (The 40-Ton Lorry)
  • Download Size: 65 Megabytes store installation.
  • Storage Demand: Requires 200MB+ for local app cache.
  • Student Behavior: Uninstalled on 16GB/32GB Tecno/itel phones.
  • Initial Data: Consumes KES 50+ in cellular data to install.
Salama Shule (The Nimble Boda-Boda)
  • Download Size: 135 Kilobytes total web payload.
  • Storage Demand: Under 2MB inside browser IndexedDB.
  • Student Behavior: Zero install; launches directly in WhatsApp.
  • Initial Data: Consumes less than 1MB of bundle data.

Module 4: Zero-Trust Client Scoring (The Bank Teller Analogy)

When a student completes an offline quiz in Salama Shule, why doesn't the backend simply trust the score calculated on their phone?

The Plain-English Mental Model: The Bank Deposit Slip

When you walk into a bank and write "KES 50,000" on a deposit slip, the teller does not credit your bank balance from your handwritten slip. The teller takes the actual banknotes and feeds them into the counting machine.

In Salama Shule, the score shown on the phone is strictly an encouragement claim. When the phone reconnects, the server takes the raw answer keys and grades them independently against master keys. If a student modified Chrome DevTools to show 100%, the server re-marks the quiz to their true score and flags the discrepancy.

Module 5: Kenyan Institutional Procurement & KDPA 2019

How do we navigate 18-month university procurement cycles and comply with student privacy laws?

Bypassing 18-Month Procurement

Public universities in Kenya are governed by PPADA 2015 tenders. Instead of selling an expensive enterprise overhaul to the University Council, we deploy as a free departmental utility for individual lecturers. Monetization occurs downstream through corporate HR verification fees (KES 250 per API call).

Cryptographic Shredding (KDPA Sec 35)

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, students have the Right to Erasure. Personal records are encrypted with a per-record key. If erasure is requested, the key is permanently destroyed. The public hash remains intact to protect registry integrity, but the identity is rendered permanently unreadable.
